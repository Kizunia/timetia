# Cancellation

- **Type:** rules
- **Module:** lecture-rescheduling
- **Status:** review
- **Owner:** ishanKulkarni13
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-27

- The faculty who teaches the [occurrence](../../../shared/glossary/occurrence.md) can **cancel it directly**, with a **mandatory reason**.
- No HOD or CR/BR approval is needed.
- Students are notified, and the ERP attendance module is informed through the `occurrence.cancelled` event.
- A cancel is an ordinary ticket and appears in the audit log.
- The HOD can cancel on behalf of an absent faculty ([faculty on leave](faculty-on-leave.md)).
