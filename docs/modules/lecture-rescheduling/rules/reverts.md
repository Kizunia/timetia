# Reverts

- **Type:** rules
- **Module:** lecture-rescheduling
- **Status:** review
- **Owner:** ishanKulkarni13
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-27

An exchange or substitute can be applied without the other faculty's consent ([approval model](approval-model.md)). The right to revert protects that faculty.

- The affected faculty can **revert at any time before the lecture starts**. Once it starts, the occurrence is fixed.
- A revert restores the original [occurrence](../../../shared/glossary/occurrence.md).
- The revert is **conflict-checked like any other change**. If the original slot has been taken meanwhile, the revert is **blocked** until the conflict is resolved.
- A revert notifies everyone who was notified of the original change.
- Every revert is written to the audit log.
