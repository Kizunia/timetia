# 0003 Self-approval with an optional reviewer checklist

- **Type:** adr
- **Module:** lecture-rescheduling
- **Status:** accepted
- **Owner:** ishanKulkarni13
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-16

## Context

Faculty coordinate in person and need a fast path, but sometimes want a second opinion.

## Decision

Faculty can apply their own change. They may also request review from any combination of the HOD, CR/BR, affected faculty and other faculty. Later refined: no case forces a review.

## Alternatives considered

Mandatory approval for every change. Rejected as too slow.

## Consequences

See [approval model](../rules/approval-model.md) and [self-approval](../../../shared/glossary/self-approval.md).

Accepted, 2026-10-16.
