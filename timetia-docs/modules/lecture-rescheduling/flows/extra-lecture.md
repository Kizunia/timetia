# Faculty adds an extra lecture

- **Type:** flow
- **Module:** lecture-rescheduling
- **Status:** review
- **Owner:** ishanKulkarni13
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-06

Migrated from the original [spec](../_archive/original-spec.md), section 8.4, and updated to the current [rules](../rules/README.md). Where they differ, the rules win.

1. Faculty chooses **Extra lecture**, picks the division or batch, the subject and a free slot.
2. The module suggests rooms that fit (type, seats, projector). The author picks the room, or sets the lecture online ([rooms](../rules/rooms-and-location.md)).
3. Conflict checks run. Every conflict blocks.
4. Review or self-approval as in [rescheduling a theory lecture](faculty-reschedule-theory.md). Extra lectures count toward faculty load as information only ([faculty load](../rules/faculty-load.md)).

A CR or BR does not raise an extra lecture ticket. They open an [issue](../rules/issues-from-cr-and-br.md) and mention the faculty, who then decides.
