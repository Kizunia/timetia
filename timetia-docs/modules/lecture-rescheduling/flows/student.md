- Sees the live timetable with changed occurrences marked.
- Receives in-app notifications for every change that affects them. Students of other divisions in shared courses are not covered in version 1 ([shared courses](../rules/shared-courses.md)).
- Sees the result of a change, not the tickets themselves ([open questions](../open-questions.md)).
- Cannot open issues.
