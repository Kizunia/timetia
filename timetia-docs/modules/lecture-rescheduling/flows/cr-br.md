- Receives changes for their division (CR, theory) or batch (BR, labs and tutorials), acknowledges them, and reviews when asked.
- May reject within scope, with a reason ([approval model](../rules/approval-model.md)).
- Opens [issues](../rules/issues-from-cr-and-br.md), for example to ask for an extra lecture. An issue does not change the timetable.
