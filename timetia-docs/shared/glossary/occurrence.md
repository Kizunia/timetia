One lecture on one specific date. The ERP timetable is a weekly pattern; an occurrence is a single dated instance of it, for example "OOP lab B2, Tue 03-Nov-2026, 10:00 to 12:00". Every change in the lecture-rescheduling module works on one occurrence, except a permanent change.
